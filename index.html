How this TingADingADing website works

1
Shared storage — roster and lineup edits are saved securely in Supabase, so approved guild leaders can work from the same data.
2
Place members — drag an unassigned member onto a slot, or tap/click the member and then a slot. Tap two filled slots to swap them.
3
Polarity Zone raids — STAR and Normal Dun are grouped into raid rows. Drag any member from a raid into its Raid Leader box, just like Guild League or Siege. Team-handle transfer still works: STAR is copied; Normal Dun teams are moved.
4
People leaving or returning — use Mark Inactive instead of deleting a member. Inactive members leave every current team and disappear from the Member Pool. Reactivate them from Dashboard when they return.
5
Website access — approved users sign in through this site. Leaders/Admins can edit; Viewers are read-only.
Export/import contains the full roster, Guild Status, membership history, Guild League, Siege, Polarity Zone, and finished-dungeon marks.
